Process

How we work

  1. Measure

    We come to the room and template it as it really is: the lean of every wall, the fall of the floor, and the services hidden behind them.

  2. Draw

    Plans and elevations show every door, panel, profile and handle. You approve them before a single board is cut, which is where changes are cheap.

  3. Build

    Everything is made, finished and dry fitted in our Mississauga shop, so problems are found on the bench instead of in your room.

  4. Install

    The same team installs, scribes to the walls and finishes in place, then hands back a room that looks as if it was always that way.

Principles

What we will not do

A few things that keep the work honest.

  • Quote per foot

    A price per linear foot describes an average room, not yours. We price from measured drawings.

  • Cut before you approve

    Nothing goes through a saw until you have signed off the drawings, finishes and hardware.

  • Hide a bad measure

    No filler strip doing the work a site measure should have done. We scribe to the wall.

  • Hand the install to strangers

    The people who built it install it, so the drawing and the room meet in one head.

Every question we get asked

Do you build in your own shop?

Yes. Everything is made and dry fitted in our shop in Mississauga before it goes to site, and the same team installs it.

Can I visit the shop?

Yes, by appointment, so someone is free to walk you through drawings, samples and the work on the bench.

Do you handle installation and finishing?

Yes. We install, scribe to the walls and finish on site rather than handing a flat pack to somebody else.

Do you work with designers, architects and contractors?

Yes. We build from design drawings, produce shop drawings for approval, and work to the contractor's schedule and site rules.
