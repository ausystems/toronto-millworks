Every question we get asked
How do you prepare a quote?
We start from your drawings, photos or dimensions, measure the room, and price from drawings that show exactly what will be built. If anything changes, the drawing and the price change together.
How much does custom millwork cost?
It depends on the size of the piece, the materials, the finish and the work inside it. We quote from measured drawings rather than a per foot rate, so the price reflects the actual room. A painted poplar built-in and a rift white oak kitchen are different jobs even at the same length.
Which areas do you serve?
Toronto and the region around it, up to about 120 km from our shop in Mississauga: Peel, Halton, Hamilton, Niagara, York, Durham, Simcoe, Dufferin, Wellington, Waterloo and Brant. Our service areas page lists every town.
Can I visit the shop?
Yes, by appointment, so someone is free to walk you through drawings, samples and the work on the bench.
Do you work with designers, architects and contractors?
Yes. We build from design drawings, produce shop drawings for approval, and work to the contractor's schedule and site rules.
