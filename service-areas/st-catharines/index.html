How a St. Catharines project runs

Every St. Catharines project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. St. Catharines is about 69 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Downtown St. Catharines, Port Dalhousie, Old Glenridge, Western Hill and Martindale?

Yes. We work across St. Catharines, including Downtown St. Catharines, Port Dalhousie, Old Glenridge, Western Hill and Martindale, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is St. Catharines from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 69 km from St. Catharines in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

Can you match the original trim in an older St. Catharines house?

Yes. We take a section from the existing profile on site, grind a knife to match and run new stock from it, so new work sits beside the original without a visible join.
