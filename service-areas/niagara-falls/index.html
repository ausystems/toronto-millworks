How a Niagara Falls project runs

Every Niagara Falls project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. Niagara Falls is about 82 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Downtown Queen Street, Stamford, Chippawa, Lundy's Lane and Fallsview?

Yes. We work across Niagara Falls, including Downtown Queen Street, Stamford, Chippawa, Lundy's Lane and Fallsview, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is Niagara Falls from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 82 km from Niagara Falls in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

Do you build restaurant and bar millwork in Niagara Falls?

Yes. Bars, back bars, counters and banquettes are shop drawn for approval, built in our shop while the space is finished, and installed in a short window.
