Milton, as it is built

Much of Milton's housing is recent, so the work is often about turning a builder's kitchen and an empty great room into something that feels designed: a proper island, a media wall, a mudroom with real storage. In Old Milton and out toward Campbellville the houses are older and the brief leans toward matching existing trim and keeping a period feel.

How a Milton project runs

Every Milton project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. Milton is about 28 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Old Milton, Timberlea, Dempsey, Beaty, Hawthorne Village and Campbellville?

Yes. We work across Milton, including Old Milton, Timberlea, Dempsey, Beaty, Hawthorne Village and Campbellville, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is Milton from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 28 km from Milton in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

What do you build most often in Milton?

Kitchens and built-in storage are the most common requests, followed by wall panelling, trim and renovations. Commercial work covers restaurants, bars and offices.
