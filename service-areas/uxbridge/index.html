How an Uxbridge project runs

Every Uxbridge project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. Uxbridge is about 62 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Uxbridge, Goodwood, Leaskdale and Zephyr?

Yes. We work across Uxbridge, including Uxbridge, Goodwood, Leaskdale and Zephyr, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is Uxbridge from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 62 km from Uxbridge in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

Do you take on larger country properties?

Yes. Farmhouse kitchens, mudrooms and libraries in homes that have often been extended more than once, which makes a careful site measure even more important.
