Collingwood, as it is built

Collingwood's chalets and lakeside homes are built for weekends with a crowd: big kitchens, mudrooms for wet gear and a lot of storage. The historic downtown along Hurontario Street has heritage buildings where restaurants and shops need millwork that suits the setting.

How a Collingwood project runs

Every Collingwood project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. Collingwood is about 100 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Downtown Collingwood, the harbour, Cranberry and the Blue Mountain area?

Yes. We work across Collingwood, including Downtown Collingwood, the harbour, Cranberry and the Blue Mountain area, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is Collingwood from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 100 km from Collingwood in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

Can you help turn a cottage into a year-round home?

Yes. Converting a cottage usually means rethinking storage, the kitchen and the trim together, in rooms that were never square, and finishes that cope with lake humidity.
