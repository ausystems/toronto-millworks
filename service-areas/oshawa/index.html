How an Oshawa project runs

Every Oshawa project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. Oshawa is about 67 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Downtown Oshawa, Northglen, Kedron, Lakeview, Eastdale and McLaughlin?

Yes. We work across Oshawa, including Downtown Oshawa, Northglen, Kedron, Lakeview, Eastdale and McLaughlin, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is Oshawa from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 67 km from Oshawa in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

What do you build most often in Oshawa?

Kitchens and built-in storage are the most common requests, followed by wall panelling, trim and renovations. Commercial work covers restaurants, bars and offices.
