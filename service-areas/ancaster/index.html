Ancaster, as it is built

Ancaster combines nineteenth century stone architecture with some of the area's larger modern homes, and both reward careful joinery. In the older houses that usually means deep window casings and trim matched to what survives. In newer homes it means scale: tall pantries, panelled studies and kitchens designed around a big family table.

How an Ancaster project runs

Every Ancaster project follows the same four stages: we measure the room as it really is, draw it for your approval, build and dry fit it in our shop, then install, scribe and finish it on site. Ancaster is about 59 km from the shop, so we book the site measure, deliveries and the install as planned trips, and the work arrives finished rather than being assembled from scratch in your room.

Every question we get asked

Do you work in Ancaster Village, Meadowlands, Fiddler's Green and the Dundas Valley edge?

Yes. We work across Ancaster, including Ancaster Village, Meadowlands, Fiddler's Green and the Dundas Valley edge, and the rest of the region within about 120 km of our shop.

How far is Ancaster from your shop?

Our shop at 2585 Drew Rd Unit 7 in Mississauga is about 59 km from Ancaster in a straight line. We plan the measure, deliveries and install so the travel never holds a job up.

Can you match the original trim in an older Ancaster house?

Yes. We take a section from the existing profile on site, grind a knife to match and run new stock from it, so new work sits beside the original without a visible join.
