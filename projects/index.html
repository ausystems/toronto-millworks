A shell, and then a room.

One commercial fit-out recorded from bare brick to opening night, and the residential joinery running alongside it.

It arrives as brick, concrete and a ceiling full of services. It leaves as somewhere you would sit down.

The fit-out, in order

Bare shell, lighting live, the feature wall in, the bar carcass against the brick, and the finished room.

The counter front

Reclaimed boards of varying tone, laid up in a running bond so no two courses line up. The top is a solid slab. Both were dry fitted on the bench before anything went to site.

The residential side

The same shop runs raised panel walls, cased openings and coffered ceilings with a gilded cornice, milled to a profile taken from the original house.
