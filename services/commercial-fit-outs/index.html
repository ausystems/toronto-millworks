Commercial Fit-Outs

Commercial Millwork and Fit-Outs

Bars, back bars, service counters, banquettes and retail joinery, built to spec and installed on a schedule that keeps the handover date.

What we build

  • Bars, back bars and service counters.
  • Banquette seating, booths and fixed furniture.
  • Host stands, retail displays and shelving systems.
  • Reception desks and office joinery.
  • Feature walls, slat walls and ceiling rafts.

Built to a programme

Commercial work lives or dies on the schedule. We build off site while the trades ahead of us finish, then install in a defined window so the space is not held open longer than it needs to be. Shop drawings go out for approval before anything is cut.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you work from an architect's drawings?

Yes. We take architectural drawings, produce shop drawings from them and submit those for approval before fabrication.

Can you work overnight to avoid closing a business?

Install windows can be scheduled around trading hours where the site and the building allow it.
